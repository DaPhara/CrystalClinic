Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know prior to your surgical or aesthetic consultation at Crystal Clinic.

Who performs the surgery at Crystal Clinic? ↓
All surgical procedures are led directly by Dr. Mam Kim Eng, board-certified plastic surgeon trained at Paul Sabatier University in France and fellowship-trained at Stanford University Hospital in the USA. We do not delegate surgical operations to unauthorized assistants.
How does Dr. Mam Kim Eng handle revision plastic surgeries? ↓
Dr. Mam Kim Eng is specialized in complex reconstructive plastic surgery. In cases of foreign body infections, crooked silicone implants, or asymmetrical eyelids performed elsewhere, she conducts thorough structural imaging, removes damaged material, reconstructs tissue using autologous cartilage or flaps, and restores a natural, healthy appearance.
How long is the typical recovery for rhinoplasty or blepharoplasty? ↓
For blepharoplasty (eyelid surgery), most patients return to normal light activities within 5 to 7 days, with suture removal on day 5 or 6. For rhinoplasty, nasal splints are removed after 7 days. Mild swelling continues to subside naturally over 2 to 4 weeks.
Can I consult via Telegram before traveling to Phnom Penh? ↓
Yes. Patients residing in other Cambodian provinces or abroad can send confidential front and profile photos to our official Telegram hotline (+855 77 656 263). Our surgical coordinators and Dr. Mam Kim Eng will evaluate feasibility and provide an initial preliminary assessment.
